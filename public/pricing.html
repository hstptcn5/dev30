What you are paying for

Dev30 does not sell AI tokens or developer scores. The paid plan unlocks the recurring product loop: authorized private context, more fresh analyses, durable history, comparison, stakeholder reports, and automation.

Usage limits

Fresh-analysis quotas are product limits, not prepaid API credits. Saved public reports can be read without consuming a fresh-analysis unit. Dev30 may adjust limits for future plans with notice before a change affects an active paid subscription.

Billing

When production billing is activated, checkout and subscription billing are processed through Paddle as Merchant of Record and synchronized to Dev30 through RevenueCat. Taxes and supported payment methods are determined during checkout.
